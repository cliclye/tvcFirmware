TIMELINE

Session

    From setup to landing.

    The short version of docs/flight-guide.md.

    The workflow

    1. Rocket setup. Enter your servos, endpoints and parts, and estimate apogee. Save.
    2. Build & flash the bench image. Power-cycle.
    3. Live board with a USB-UART: detect the nose axis, then do the tilt test. When the nose leans one way, the nozzle must swing the opposite way. Fix channels and directions in Rocket setup, save, rebuild, reflash.
    4. Launch checklist: finish the bench section, then flash the flight image.
    5. At the pad: power on, hands off. Solid green and a chirp every second means ARMED. Red, or a long beep every 2 s, means don't launch.
    6. Flight logs: put the SD card in your Mac and read the session.

    Safety

    Use 2S LiPo only (the servo rail is a linear 5 V regulator). Never connect pyro channel P2. Nothing here has flown before your first flight, so treat it as a test flight: clear area, low wind, a stand that holds the rocket vertical.

    Tools you'll need on the Mac

    brew install cmake dfu-util. The ARM compiler is in the project's .tools folder.
